Tips for a Better Score

  • Use a clean, single-column format for ATS compatibility
  • Include quantifiable achievements with numbers and percentages
  • Add relevant keywords from your target job descriptions
  • Keep resume to 1 page for under 2 years of experience
  • List skills in a dedicated section at the top
  • Include links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and live projects
  • Use action verbs: Built, Developed, Designed, Improved
